Try something random, and find an opportunity

In October 2024, my wife and I rescued a dog in Peru
I arrived to Sydney July 2025 and as we missed having our dog around in Peru, we decided why not try petsitting
I open a profile in Pawshake and Madpaws, to do pet sitting, with average experience but much love.
Quickly I notice that there was high demand of this service, and still many problems to solve for sitters/owners

While taking care of dogs we discover many opportunities in workflow:
- Entry Form
- Dog Database
- Calendar Status
- AI isted response / advice

I decided to create a platform that could address this kind of problems. Then we got Pawsie.
